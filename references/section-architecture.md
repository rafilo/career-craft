# Section architecture

Choose structure by evidence and target, not by habit.

## Early career or graduate

1. Header and target title
2. Professional summary
3. Core skills
4. Projects or relevant experience
5. Employment
6. Education
7. Certifications/activities when useful

Move Education above Projects when the qualification is the primary credential.

## Experienced individual contributor

1. Header and target title
2. Professional summary
3. Core capabilities/technical skills
4. Professional experience
5. Selected projects only when they add evidence not visible in Experience
6. Education
7. Certifications

## Technical leader or manager

1. Header and leadership target
2. Executive/professional summary
3. Leadership and technical capabilities
4. Professional experience
5. Selected achievements only when this prevents important evidence being buried
6. Education and certifications

Avoid detaching achievements from employers if that makes evidence ambiguous.

## Career changer

1. Header and target title
2. Transition summary grounded in transferable capability
3. Relevant skills
4. Relevant projects, training, or selected experience
5. Broader employment history
6. Education/certifications

Do not hide the career change. Make the bridge credible.

## QA or test professional

Prioritise test planning, requirements/user stories, acceptance criteria, manual and
automation testing, API/data validation, defect lifecycle, stakeholder communication,
SDLC/Agile delivery, and user-focused quality. Keep tool lists subordinate to testing
capability and outcomes.

## Optional sections

Include Publications, Awards, Community Contribution, Volunteering, Languages, or
Work Rights only when useful for the target application or local context.

## Merge/remove guidance

- Merge overlapping Highlights, Strengths, and Skills sections.
- Remove generic Values or Personal Attributes sections when they repeat evidence.
- Remove References Available on Request unless explicitly expected.
- Compress old unrelated roles into one or two bullets rather than deleting career
  continuity without explanation.
