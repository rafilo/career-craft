# Resume quality checklist

## Positioning

- Is the target role obvious within the first screen/page?
- Does the summary describe this candidate rather than a generic applicant?
- Is seniority consistent with the evidence?
- Are strongest relevant capabilities visible early?

## Evidence

- Does each recent role show meaningful contributions, scope, or purpose?
- Are achievements distinguishable from routine responsibilities?
- Are all numbers and outcomes traceable to the source?
- Are collaboration and ownership claims supported?
- Do bullets clarify the candidate's individual contribution rather than only the
  team's responsibility?
- Are metrics verified rather than forced into every bullet?
- Where numbers are unavailable, is there other concrete evidence such as scope,
  delivery, adoption, quality, risk reduction, or stakeholder use?

## Relevance

- Is detail weighted toward the target role?
- Are older or unrelated roles compressed appropriately?
- Are transferable skills made explicit without pretending to have direct experience?
- Are unsupported JD keywords excluded?

## Writing

- Are bullets concise, varied, grammatical, and parallel?
- Is tense consistent: present for current work, past for completed work?
- Are filler phrases and repeated claims removed?
- Is terminology and capitalisation consistent?
- Is New Zealand/British English used by default?

## Skills

- Are skills grouped and prioritised meaningfully?
- Are practical skills separated from learning or exposure where necessary?
- Is the list consistent with experience evidence?
- Are soft skills demonstrated rather than merely asserted?

## ATS and layout

- Are headings conventional and descriptive?
- Is important information represented as selectable text?
- Are contact details outside essential headers/footers?
- Are dates unambiguous and consistently aligned?
- Is body text comfortably readable?
- Are page breaks intentional?

## Final integrity check

- No fabricated metrics, skills, leadership, qualifications, or company facts.
- No placeholder or `[VERIFY]` marker remains in the submission version.
- No spelling, grammar, date, or title inconsistency remains.
- Links, email address, and phone number are accurate.

## Coaching output check

- Did the response explain why the issue matters?
- Did it ask only focused, answerable evidence questions?
- Did it give the user a structure to try before showing the finished answer?
- Did feedback identify what the user's own revision improved?
- Did the final demonstration use only confirmed facts?
