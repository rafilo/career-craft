# Truthfulness and evidence policy

## Never invent

- Metrics, percentages, revenue, user counts, time savings, or performance gains
- Technologies, platforms, methodologies, or certifications
- Leadership, ownership, direct reports, budgets, or decision authority
- Promotions, awards, publications, client names, or regulated-domain experience
- Employment dates, titles, education results, or work rights

## Safe transformations

- Correct grammar and terminology.
- Make an explicit responsibility more concise.
- Convert passive wording to active wording without increasing scope.
- Draw a restrained capability statement from repeated evidence.
- Reorder existing facts to match job relevance.

## Unsafe transformations

- Turning `participated in` into `led`.
- Turning `helped test` into `owned QA strategy`.
- Turning `worked with AWS` into `architected cloud platforms`.
- Adding a numerical improvement because a bullet feels weak.
- Copying requirements from a JD into the resume as candidate experience.

## Handling gaps

If a stronger version needs missing evidence, retain a truthful neutral version and
add a separate prompt such as:

`[VERIFY: Was there a measurable reduction in processing time or support volume?]`

Do not leave `[VERIFY]` markers inside a submission-ready final unless the user asks
for an annotated draft.
