# ATS and layout rules

## Content and parsing

- Use standard headings such as Professional Summary, Skills, Experience,
  Education, Certifications, and Projects.
- Keep employer, title, location, and dates as real text.
- Avoid placing essential information in headers, footers, images, icons, charts,
  or text boxes.
- Avoid skill-rating bars, dot scales, star ratings, and logos.
- Spell out uncommon abbreviations on first use.
- Use exact JD terminology only when factually supported.

## A4 layout

- Page size: A4 (210 × 297 mm).
- Default margins: 13–17 mm; never sacrifice readability merely to fit one page.
- Body text: approximately 9.5–11 pt; headings 12–16 pt; name 20–28 pt.
- Line height: approximately 1.25–1.45.
- Use one professional font family or a restrained two-font pairing.
- Use consistent vertical spacing and date alignment.
- Keep a heading with at least the first item that follows it.
- Avoid a single orphan bullet at the top of a page where practical.

## Length

- One page: early-career candidates or short, tightly relevant histories.
- Two pages: experienced candidates with relevant evidence.
- More than two pages only when the domain, seniority, academic record, or user
  requirement justifies it.

## Visual hierarchy

Colour, weight, spacing, and alignment should clarify information, not decorate it.
Maintain sufficient contrast. Use an accent colour sparingly for headings, rules,
or small labels, never for long body text.
