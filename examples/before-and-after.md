# Before-and-after examples

These examples demonstrate editing patterns, not reusable candidate facts.

## Personal statement

Before:

> I am a full-stack software engineer. I have worked in startups and large companies
> and I am flexible and an effective problem solver. I can manage a project from
> start to end and I also have experience leading a technical team.

After:

> Full-stack software engineer experienced in delivering applications across both
> start-up and enterprise environments. Comfortable owning work from stakeholder
> discovery and technical design through implementation, release, and production
> support. Combines hands-on engineering with clear technical leadership, helping
> teams resolve ambiguity, make pragmatic decisions, and maintain delivery momentum.

Why it works: it preserves the source facts, strengthens flow and specificity, and
does not invent scale, metrics, or technologies.

## Experience bullet

Before:

> Responsible for APIs and worked with the frontend team to fix issues.

Coach before rewriting:

- Which APIs did you personally develop or maintain?
- What kind of integration issue did you diagnose?
- What did resolving it enable: a release, fewer failures, or clearer integration?
- Is there evidence, such as a shipped feature or reduced support work?

After when no outcome is supplied:

> Developed and maintained APIs, collaborating with frontend engineers to diagnose
> integration issues and support reliable feature delivery.

Do not upgrade this to `led API architecture` or add a percentage improvement.

The purpose of the coaching questions is to give the candidate a chance to add real
evidence. The After example is a demonstration only when no further evidence exists.

## AI capability

Weak:

> AI enthusiast who uses AI tools.

Stronger when supported:

> Applies AI across the software delivery lifecycle, using large language models for
> requirements analysis, implementation support, test design, debugging, and
> documentation, with practical experience designing agentic workflows.

The final wording must reflect what that candidate has actually done.

## QA AI usage

> Use Claude Code to analyse user stories, support test planning and bug
> investigation, and assist with drafting and refining Playwright automation code.

This works because it names the tool, workflow stages, and practical purpose.

## Responsibility to contribution

Before:

> Responsible for onboarding new team members.

Ask first:

- Did you create the onboarding programme or deliver an existing one?
- How many people, teams, or locations were involved?
- What changed in readiness, consistency, or time to productivity?

Possible version only if the facts are confirmed:

> Designed and scaled an onboarding programme for more than 20 new hires across
> three offices, reducing average ramp-up time by two weeks.

Do not borrow the numbers from this example for another candidate.
